## Direction contract

THESIS: One shell for anime, film and TV where resume state leads. Refuses the
mode-hub landing screen and the left rail that split the app into two products.

OWN-WORLD: Cool near-black #0B0D13 ground, #151824 surfaces, one #0063E5
accent, #F5F7FA text. Top navigation bar over full-bleed key art; edge-to-edge
poster rows; 8px radii; scrims only over artwork, never gradients as ornament;
no glassmorphism.

STORY: You open it already knowing what you are part-way through, resume in one
click, and reach library and discovery as peers from one nav.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed hero at ~62vh carrying key art behind a
left-weighted scrim. Title, meta row, then Resume and My List. The top nav
floats transparent over it. A 16:9 Continue Watching row breaks the fold.

FORM: The category standard played straight — the standing exit, taken in the
user's own words over the roll's assignment. Seed key 5b3359dd. Craft bar:
Disney+, Apple TV+, Max.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
